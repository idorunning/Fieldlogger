# App access and reviewer notes

The native app opens to a dated scrapbook. Core camera capture, local journal, notes, collection, milestones, archiving and sharing work without an account. Tap **Camera** at the bottom. Grant Camera and foreground Location when Android first prompts; subsequent captures reuse the grants automatically. The journal and camera can save offline.

For sync, use the account icon → **Sign in or register** and register a normal password account, or supply a dedicated reviewer account privately in Play Console. Do not use your personal credentials in public listing files. Login uses My Trail Log only.

Identification and publishing use the shared server service automatically for every signed-in account. There is no credential entry in Android. The saved shared key was tested live with GPT-6.1 Sol for identification and publishing checks. New accounts use it automatically; users do not need their own key. No reviewer credential has been created or placed in public files.

Swipe a day's overlapping photos horizontally to turn the stack; scroll vertically to another day. Tap a photo for notes and **Photo & story**, which previews a branded image before Android sharing. **Move to archive** keeps the memory; account icon → **Archive** restores it. Archived records are excluded from active map and counts and remain in backups.

Account settings provide export/import and deletion. Public deletion URL: https://fieldlogger.co.uk/delete-account. Use the same account on the website and confirm the password and checkbox.

Suggested App access declaration: some functionality requires access instructions, because private account sync and configured identification need an account. Core device-local features remain freely accessible.


Community: open a journal photo → Publish discovery. Accept the sharing guidelines and choose Everyone, Local (map centre and radius) or Specific people (email invite links). Tap a photo’s acorn to like it. Map → Following/Nearby/Everyone/Later/Invited filters shared discoveries. Select a public username to follow or open its shared diary. Report and Block are in shared-photo details. Profile and settings → Profile & privacy manages username, optional email discovery and blocks; My published photos includes Unpublish. A private invite opens only after its intended account accepts the link.

2.3.0: the avatar in the journal header opens Profile and settings. Journal has Sync now, Archive, existing-photo import and backups. App preferences includes weather and follower notifications. Friends & contacts offers optional Contacts permission followed by an explicit address selection screen. My published photos includes Publish all (choose audience and confirm) and Unpublish all (warning). The map search icon opens postcode/place lookup and photo-name filters; Filters includes Trees and most photographed areas. Milestones shows the permanent woodland levels and date-specific badges.
