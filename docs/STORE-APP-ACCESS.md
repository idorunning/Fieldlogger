# Play reviewer access — My Trail Log 2.4.0

Package `com.field.logger`, version code **11**. This is native Android, with no browser journal wrapper. Company site: https://fieldlogger.co.uk. Separate browser journal: https://fieldlogger.co.uk/journal.

## Local journal

Open the dated scrapbook and tap **Camera** in the bottom bar. Camera/foreground Location permissions are optional and reused after granting. Photos save offline; swipe overlapping cards through a day and scroll between days. Notes, collection, permanent milestones, archive/restore, sharing and local backup work without a subscription. GPS can be absent if denied/unavailable. No walk-distance or continuous location tracking.

Tap a photo → **Photo & story** to preview a branded share image. Place can be hidden; exact GPS/EXIF are omitted. Settings → **Archive** restores memories. Archives retain data.

## Account and shared service

Avatar → **Sign in or register** uses a normal password account. The browser journal uses the same account. Supply a dedicated reviewer account privately in Play Console if required; no reviewer or administrator credential is included in public files.

Identification and publication checks use the operator's shared server service automatically. Android has no service-key entry. Ordinary recognition uses an efficient pass; harder/uncertain photos can receive an automatic stronger **Closer Look** within a separate plan allowance. Without review, the result remains broad/tentative. AI suggestions and confidence labels are not verified species accuracy. Availability, allowance and abuse/spending limits apply; local photos remain safe after failures.

Settings groups profile, community, journal, preferences and help/account. **Sync now**, **Archive**, import and exports are in Journal. Weather/effects and follower alerts are optional. Contacts review sends only selected addresses after explicit confirmation.

## Avatar

**Make my avatar** offers illustrated options and optional photo-based cartoon creation. Taking/choosing a portrait requires an explicit permission and OpenAI-processing confirmation. Source EXIF/GPS is stripped; My Trail Log does not save/publish the source portrait. Only a checked generated cartoon becomes the profile avatar. OpenAI retention terms still apply.

A free account has one initial processing attempt; an active subscription/trial has one per UTC month. A submitted provider attempt can use the allowance even if it fails. Service/budget limits can defer creation; existing avatars remain usable.

## Community and map

Photo → **Publish discovery** requires sharing agreement and audience: Everyone, Local (map centre/radius), or Specific people (invites). Photos with people or uncertain/failed checks stay private. Following never bypasses private invitations. Acorns/counts appear at the lower-right corner. Report/Block are available in shared-photo details.

Map filters: My photos, Following, Nearby, Everyone, Later and Invited. Search postcode/place, photo names/types or trees, sort by acorns, or see photographed areas within available results. Photo markers merge into numbered clusters when zooming out and split when zooming in. Save discoveries for later. **Publish all** requires audience/consent/confirmation; **Unpublish all** shows a warning.

Milestones contains **352 permanent achievements**, with colour-coded Soil, Clay, Flint, Quartz, Amber and Gold layers. Species/time/place/date challenges reveal progressively, including calendar-day badges.
